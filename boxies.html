Boxies

Magda makes a range of “Boxies” (that's what she likes to call them, other people call them all sorts of things: utility pouches, makeup / cosmetic bags, toiletry cases, hairdryer covers, travel / shoe bags ….). Depending on size they have a wide range of uses, they're washable gently and all fully lined with water resistant fabric.

Boxies are available in four sizes:

S - Small
M - Medium
L - Large
XL - Extra Large

S - Small

L 215 x W 115 x H 90 mm

M - Medium

L 215 x W 115 x H 90 mm

L - Large

L 215 x W 115 x H 90 mm

XL - Extra Large

L 215 x W 115 x H 90 mm

Please note: since all Boxies are handmade the above measurements may vary slightly – that's what is special about handmade, no two items are exactly the same.

To order on line, choose your preferred size(s) (sets of 2, 3 or 4 sizes are available) and fabric, then head over to our Shop. If you want to choose more than one boxy with different fabrics, you'll need to do that as separate orders - send us a message first.

There is always a selection of boxies on offer at our popup shop at Evandale Sunday Market – we're there every Sunday (weather permitting – you can always check here) so come and see us there if your don't want to order online.
