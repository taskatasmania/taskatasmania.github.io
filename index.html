Market Information

We are really proud to be hosted by the Evandale Sunday Market, Tasmania's longest-running and largest Sunday market. You can find us at the Collins St / Huxtables Lane end of the Market (at that Falls Park sign) every Sunday* (enter from Logan Rd or the carpark).

Evandale is a picturesque and historic village in Northern Tasmania, 20 min drive from central regional hubs and 7 min from the airport. It's home to the renowned Glover Prize for landscape painting, the annual National Penny Farthing Championships and much more.
