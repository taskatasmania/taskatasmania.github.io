Boxies

Magda makes a range of “Boxies” (that's what she likes to call them, elsewhere they can be: utility pouches, makeup / cosmetic bags, toiletry cases, hairdryer covers, travel / shoe bags ….). Depending on size they have a wide range of uses, they're gentle washable and all fully lines with water resistant fabric.

The Boxies are available in four sizes:

S - Small M - Medium L - Large XL - Extra Large
L 215 x W 115 x H 90 mm [M Data] [L Data] [XL Data]

Please note: since all Boxies are handmade the sizes above may vary slightly – that's what is special about handmade, no two items are exactly the same.
